### Core Contributions

We present a minimal axiom system for causal composition, fully formalized in
Lean~4 with Mathlib (2089 jobs, 0 errors, 0 \texttt{sorry}). From four
elementary axioms---a total binary composition, its associativity, an exact
additivity law for dependency lists, and a no-duplicate constraint---we prove
three main results:

1. **Core Collapse**: Every rule has an empty dependency list as a necessary
   consequence of the axioms alone. We further prove that associativity is
   redundant for this result (three axioms suffice), and that neither exact
   additivity nor no-duplicate can be weakened.

2. **Finite-Free Two-Aspect Dichotomy**: The dichotomy between amplitude
   injectivity and output constancy holds without any finiteness assumption on
   the type of rules, improving on prior formalizations that required
   \texttt{[Finite C]}.

3. **Unique Prime Basis**: The first four terms of the sequence
   $s_0 = 2$, $s_{n+1} = 3 + 2n$ generate the prime basis $\{2,3,5,7\}$, which
   is uniquely determined by the prime factors of the orders of three finite
   simple groups $A_4$, $A_5$, and $\mathrm{PSL}(2,7)$. Each prime is shown to
   be necessary by an exclusion argument.

### Suitability for JAR

Given JAR's focus on the theory, implementation, and applications of formal
proof assistants, we believe this work is well-suited. The paper demonstrates:

- A machine-verified minimal axiom system and its structural consequences,
  directly aligning with the journal's interest in automated reasoning and
  formal verification.
- An elementary theorem (Core Collapse) whose proof requires only the
  \texttt{Nodup} property on lists---accessible to a broad JAR readership.
- A hierarchy of results from the most concrete (Core Collapse) to the
  most abstract (finite-free dichotomy), providing a complete narrative.
