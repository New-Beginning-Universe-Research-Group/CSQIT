# Cover Letter for Submission

**To the Editor of Physical Review D**

Dear Editor,

I am pleased to submit my paper "CSQIT: Causal Structure Quantum Information Theory—From Axioms to Observable Cosmology" for consideration for publication in Physical Review D.

This work presents a fundamentally new approach to theoretical physics: a fully formalized discrete causal-information axiomatic framework implemented in Lean 4. What distinguishes this work is the combination of three key features:

**1. Zero-Parameter Deductive Chain**

Starting from a small set of information-theoretic axioms (AxiomA–K), I derive through machine-verifiable proofs a complete deductive chain from axioms to observable cosmology. A characteristic constant θ = 1/(2+2cos(2π/7)) ≈ 0.308 emerges naturally from the algebraic structure of cyclic group Fin 7. This derivation involves no free parameters—θ is uniquely determined by the axiom system.

**2. Formalized Verification**

All mathematical claims in this work are implemented and verified in Lean 4. The complete proof code consists of 2069 compilation tasks, all passing with zero errors. This level of formalization ensures that the deductive chain is mathematically rigorous and free from implicit assumptions.

**3. Empirical Anchor**

Under the duality interpretation, the derived constant θ corresponds to the observed total matter density Ω_m = 0.311 (Planck 2018) with a deviation of approximately 1%. While this correspondence is a physical interpretation (W2/W3 level), not a mathematical theorem (W1 level), it provides an empirical anchor that suggests the framework may be relevant to real physics.

**Key Contributions**:

1. A complete axiomatic system (AxiomA–K) formalized in Lean 4
2. The Duality Two-One Theorem: a discrete complementarity principle
3. Algebraic causal order: causal relations derived from algebraic structure
4. A complete deductive chain from axioms to θ with zero free parameters
5. An empirical anchor connecting the framework to observational cosmology

**Comparison with Existing Approaches**:

CSQIT differs from string theory, loop quantum gravity, and causal set theory in that it:
- Uses formalized verification to eliminate implicit assumptions
- Produces a quantitative result (θ) comparable to observational data
- Requires no free parameters

It demonstrates that the axiomatic-formalized approach can produce empirically relevant results—a methodology that could transform how theoretical physics is conducted.
