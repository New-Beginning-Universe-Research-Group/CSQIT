**2. Algebraic Uniqueness of p=7**

The algebraic expansion spectrum θ(p) = 1/(2+2cos(2π/p)) is strictly monotonically decreasing for primes p ≥ 3. Only p=7 yields θ(p) within the structure formation window (0.28, 0.33): p=3 produces θ=1.0 (static), p=5 produces θ=0.382 (too high), and p≥11 produces θ<0.272 (too low for galaxy formation).

**Key Contribution**: This is the first implementation of a complete deductive chain from information-theoretic axioms to observable cosmological parameters with zero free parameters, verified by a proof assistant.
